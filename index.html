About me

I'm Abhishek, a counsellor and mental health advocate based in Indore. I understand how difficult it can feel to navigate social anxiety, second-guess yourself, or be your own harshest critic.

My approach begins with listening, understanding your experiences, and creating a space where you feel comfortable being yourself. Together, we'll explore what's weighing on you, build practical tools you can use in everyday life, and work towards greater confidence and self-understanding, at a pace that feels right for you.
