What we can work on

Every session is private and shaped around what you need right now.

General anxiety

Understand your triggers and learn practical tools to calm racing thoughts.

Clarity

Self-esteem

About me

I'm Abhishek, a counsellor and mental health advocate based in Indore. I hold an MA in Counselling Psychology and am certified in Adult Mental Health First Aid (USA). I know how heavy it can feel to be nervous around others or to be hard on yourself.

In our sessions I listen first, ask careful questions and share practical tools you can use between meetings, always at a pace that suits you.
